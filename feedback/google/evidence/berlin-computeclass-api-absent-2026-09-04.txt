  CONCLUSION: the ComputeClass API is not served in this cluster, so the
  custom-ComputeClass route that the autogke-gpu-limitation message offers as an
  alternative is not available to us. That leaves only the

  probe variants 1 and 2 -- and which never provisions a node.
