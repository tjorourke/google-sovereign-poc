## Three things that had to be right, each failed alone first
1. PVC for the weights. HF caches to the container filesystem; Autopilot
   gives 1Gi by default and the NODE only allocates ~47GB, against ~62GB
   of Gemma 3 27B. Exceeding it evicts rather than fails, restarts stay 0.
2. memory 128Gi, not 32Gi. vLLM materialises weights in CPU RAM before
   moving them to the GPU. 32Gi = OOM kill, exit 137, five minutes in,
   AFTER the download completed.
3. --dtype=bfloat16. vLLM downcasts to float16 by default; Gemma is
   trained in bf16 and overflows in fp16. Silent: /v1/models answers, the
   request returns 200 with a token count, and content is an empty string.
