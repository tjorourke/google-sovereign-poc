07
Data protection

Masked before any model sees it

An open-source DLP (Presidio) runs in the cluster. Customer and restricted data is replaced with placeholders and answered only by Gemma in Berlin; secrets and prompt injection are refused. Upload a document — it is opened in-cluster and summarised without leaving the boundary.
