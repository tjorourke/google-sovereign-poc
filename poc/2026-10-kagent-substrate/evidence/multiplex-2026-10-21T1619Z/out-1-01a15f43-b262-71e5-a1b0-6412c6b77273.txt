A bank cares where its AI agents run because data security, regulatory compliance, and operational reliability are paramount. Running AI agents on trusted, secure infrastructure ensures sensitive financial data is protected against breaches. Compliance with banking regulations often requires data to stay within specific geographic regions, like Germany or the EU. Moreover, trusted environments minimize downtime and ensure consistent performance, which is vital for customer trust and service continuity. Thus, the location and platform for AI agents directly impact a bank’s risk management and reputation.
