A bank cares where its AI agents run to ensure security, data privacy, and regulatory compliance. Banks handle sensitive financial and personal information, so hosting AI agents in trusted, secure environments—such as dedicated sovereign cloud platforms—minimizes risks of data breaches and unauthorized access. Regulatory frameworks often require data to reside within specific jurisdictions like Germany or the EU, to protect customer rights and adhere to laws. Additionally, trusted environments provide reliability and performance critical for banking operations and customer trust.
