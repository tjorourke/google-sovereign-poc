A bank cares where its AI agents run because security, compliance, and data privacy are critical. Running AI agents on trusted, sovereign platforms ensures sensitive financial data stays within controlled jurisdictions, protecting against unauthorized access and regulatory breaches. It also guarantees adherence to strict banking regulations and audit requirements. Performance and reliability of the infrastructure are vital too, as customer services must be fast and dependable. Overall, location impacts legal, operational, and reputational risks, making it a top priority for banks.
