A bank cares where its AI agents run primarily for security, compliance, and data privacy reasons. Financial institutions handle sensitive customer data, so running AI agents in a secure, controlled environment ensures protection against breaches. Regulatory requirements mandate that data remain within specific jurisdictions, such as within Germany or the EU, to comply with data protection laws like GDPR. Additionally, running AI on trusted infrastructure, like Google Cloud Dedicated in Berlin for TrustUsBank, helps guarantee reliability, auditability, and operational control, all crucial for banking trustworthiness.
