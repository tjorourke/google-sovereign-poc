A bank cares where its AI agents run because of security, compliance, and performance reasons. Financial data is highly sensitive and regulated, so AI systems must operate in secure, controlled environments to prevent data breaches. Banks must comply with local laws on data privacy and processing, often requiring data to stay within specific jurisdictions like Germany. Running AI agents on trusted, dedicated infrastructure ensures reliability, low latency, and alignment with the bank’s strict operational standards, supporting safe, efficient financial services.
