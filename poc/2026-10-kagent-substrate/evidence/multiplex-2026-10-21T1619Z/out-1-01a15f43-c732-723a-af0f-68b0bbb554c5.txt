A bank cares where its AI agents run because security and compliance are paramount. Running AI within a trusted, controlled environment helps protect sensitive financial data from unauthorized access or breaches. It ensures adherence to regulatory requirements, such as data sovereignty laws that mandate data to remain within specific jurisdictions. Additionally, reliable infrastructure minimizes downtime and errors, safeguarding customer trust. Ultimately, the location and platform impact operational risk, privacy, and regulatory compliance, all critical for a bank's reputation and legal standing.
