A bank cares deeply about where its AI agents run to ensure security, privacy, and regulatory compliance. Running AI on trusted, controlled infrastructure minimizes risks of data breaches and unauthorized access to sensitive financial and personal data. It also helps meet strict legal requirements around data residency and protection. Moreover, robust and transparent AI deployment supports auditability and accountability, which are critical in financial services to maintain customer trust and avoid costly penalties. Thus, location and environment for AI agents are vital for safe, compliant banking operations.
