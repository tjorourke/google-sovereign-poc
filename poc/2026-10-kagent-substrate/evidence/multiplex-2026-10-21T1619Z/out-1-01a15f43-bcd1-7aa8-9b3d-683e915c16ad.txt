A bank cares where its AI agents run primarily for security, compliance, and data privacy reasons. Financial institutions handle sensitive customer data and must follow strict regulations, especially in jurisdictions like Germany. Running AI agents on trusted, sovereign platforms controlled by the bank or approved providers ensures data stays within regulatory boundaries and reduces risks of breaches. Additionally, hosting AI in secure environments enables better control, monitoring, and auditing, which supports trustworthiness, operational integrity, and adherence to legal requirements in banking.
