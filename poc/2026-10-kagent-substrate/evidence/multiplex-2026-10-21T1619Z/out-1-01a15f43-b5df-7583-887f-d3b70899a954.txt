A bank cares where its AI agents run because data security and regulatory compliance are paramount. Banks handle sensitive financial and personal customer information, which must be protected from breaches. Running AI agents within trusted, secure environments reduces risk. Additionally, banks must comply with strict laws on data residency and privacy, often requiring data to stay within specific jurisdictions. Hosting AI agents in controlled, audited infrastructures, like dedicated cloud platforms, ensures trustworthiness, performance reliability, and adherence to legal and operational standards.
