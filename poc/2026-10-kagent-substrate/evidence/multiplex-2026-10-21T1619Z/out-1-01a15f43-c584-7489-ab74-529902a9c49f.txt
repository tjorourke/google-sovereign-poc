A bank cares where its AI agents run primarily for security, compliance, and data privacy reasons. Financial data is highly sensitive, so running AI within trusted, secure environments protects against cyber threats and unauthorized access. Banks must comply with regulations like GDPR and local banking laws, often requiring data to stay within specific geographic regions, such as Germany or the EU. Additionally, trusted platforms ensure reliability and auditability of AI operations, fostering customer trust and meeting internal risk management standards.
