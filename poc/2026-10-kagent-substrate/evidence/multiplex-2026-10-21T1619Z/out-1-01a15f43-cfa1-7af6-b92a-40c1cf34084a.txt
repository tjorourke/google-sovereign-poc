A bank cares about where its AI agents run because security, compliance, and data privacy are critical in banking. Running AI agents on trusted, secure infrastructure like a sovereign platform in a specific location (e.g., Berlin) ensures control over data residency and adherence to strict regulations such as GDPR. It also helps prevent unauthorized access and cyber threats. Reliable infrastructure supports stable performance and auditability, which are essential for maintaining customer trust, regulatory compliance, and protecting sensitive financial data.
