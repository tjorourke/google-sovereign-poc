Banks care where their AI agents run because of strict regulatory, security, and privacy requirements. They must ensure customer data is protected according to laws like GDPR. Running AI agents on trusted, secure infrastructure prevents data breaches and unauthorized access. Location matters for compliance with local data residency rules and for minimizing latency in financial transactions. Using controlled environments like dedicated cloud platforms with strong safeguards maintains trust, reduces operational risks, and aligns with the bank’s duty to protect sensitive financial information.
