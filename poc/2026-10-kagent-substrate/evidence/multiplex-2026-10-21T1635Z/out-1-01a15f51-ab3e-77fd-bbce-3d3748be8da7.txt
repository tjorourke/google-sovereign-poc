A bank cares where its AI agents run for security, compliance, and data privacy reasons. Running AI agents within a controlled and secure environment, such as an on-premises or trusted cloud in the bank's jurisdiction, reduces risks of data breaches and unauthorized access. It ensures regulatory compliance with local laws on data handling and financial services. Location also affects latency and performance, impacting real-time decision-making and customer experience. Ultimately, safe and compliant deployment protects the bank’s assets and reputation.
