A bank cares where its AI agents run primarily due to data security, regulatory compliance, and operational reliability. Banking data is highly sensitive and subject to strict privacy laws, especially in Germany and the EU. Running AI agents within trusted, local infrastructure like a sovereign platform ensures control over data flow and reduces cyber risks. Additionally, complying with jurisdictional regulations and achieving low latency for financial operations are crucial. Secure, dedicated environments also enable better monitoring and incident response, protecting both the bank and its customers.
