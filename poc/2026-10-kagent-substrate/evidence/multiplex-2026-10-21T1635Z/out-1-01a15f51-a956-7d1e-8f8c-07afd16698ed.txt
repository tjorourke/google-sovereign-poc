A bank cares where its AI agents run because security, compliance, and data privacy are paramount. Running AI within a trusted, controlled environment like a dedicated sovereign platform ensures customer data is protected from unauthorized access and cyber threats. It also helps meet regulatory requirements specific to the banking sector and jurisdiction, such as GDPR in Germany. Reliable infrastructure reduces risks of downtime and errors, maintaining trust and operational continuity. Location and platform influence the bank's ability to enforce strict governance over sensitive financial processes.
