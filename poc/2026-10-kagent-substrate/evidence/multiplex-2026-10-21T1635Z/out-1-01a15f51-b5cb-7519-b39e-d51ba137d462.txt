A bank cares deeply where its AI agents run because security and compliance are paramount. Running AI within a trusted, sovereign cloud environment ensures sensitive financial data is protected against breaches and meets strict regulatory standards. It also guarantees data residency laws are respected, reducing legal risks. A controlled environment allows the bank to monitor, audit, and manage AI behaviors closely, safeguarding customer trust and operational integrity. Ultimately, location impacts reliability, confidentiality, and compliance, which are critical for banking institutions.
