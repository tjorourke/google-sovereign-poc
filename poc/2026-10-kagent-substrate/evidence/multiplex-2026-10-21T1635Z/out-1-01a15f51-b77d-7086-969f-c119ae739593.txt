A bank cares where its AI agents run to ensure data security, regulatory compliance, and operational reliability. Since banks handle sensitive financial and personal data, AI processing must occur in secure environments with strong privacy controls. Running AI agents within trusted, sovereign platforms—like local cloud data centers—helps safeguard against unauthorized access and data breaches. It also aligns with legal requirements for data residency and protection. Additionally, choosing reliable infrastructure minimizes downtime, ensuring consistent and trusted banking services for customers.
