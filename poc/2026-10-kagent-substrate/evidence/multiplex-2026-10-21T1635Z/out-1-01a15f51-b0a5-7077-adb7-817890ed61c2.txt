A bank cares where its AI agents run primarily due to security, privacy, and regulatory compliance. Financial institutions handle sensitive customer data and financial transactions, so running AI agents in secure, controlled environments reduces risks of data breaches and cyberattacks. Banks must comply with strict data protection laws and regulations that often require data to remain within specific geographic locations. Using trusted, sovereign platforms ensures transparency, auditability, and adherence to these rules, maintaining customer trust and avoiding legal penalties.
