A bank cares where its AI agents run primarily for security, compliance, and control. Running AI agents in a trusted, secure environment helps protect sensitive financial data from breaches. Regulatory requirements mandate data residency and privacy controls, so banks must ensure AI processing aligns with legal standards. The bank also needs dependable performance and operational reliability, which are easier to guarantee in dedicated, carefully managed infrastructure. Ultimately, controlling the runtime environment reduces risks and ensures the AI supports banking operations safely and effectively.
