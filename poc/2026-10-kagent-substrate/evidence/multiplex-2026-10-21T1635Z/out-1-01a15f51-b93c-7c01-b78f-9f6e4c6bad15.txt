A bank cares where its AI agents run because data security, privacy, and regulatory compliance are critical in banking. Running AI on trusted, secure infrastructure—such as a sovereign platform within the bank's jurisdiction—ensures sensitive customer and transaction data is protected against unauthorized access. It supports adherence to data protection laws like GDPR, prevents data leakage, and maintains the bank's reputation. Additionally, control over the AI environment allows for reliable performance and easier auditing, essential for risk management and trustworthiness in financial services.
