A bank cares where its AI agents run because of security, compliance, and data privacy. Hosting AI agents within controlled environments like a sovereign platform ensures sensitive financial data is protected against unauthorized access and cyber threats. Regulatory requirements demand strict data residency and handling protocols that must be met. Running AI agents on trusted infrastructure, such as a dedicated cloud in a defined location, supports auditability, reduces risk, and helps maintain customer trust by safeguarding their confidential information effectively.
