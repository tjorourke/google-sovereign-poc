A bank cares where its AI agents run primarily for security, compliance, and data privacy reasons. Running AI within a trusted, sovereign platform like a dedicated cloud in Berlin ensures control over sensitive financial data, protecting it from unauthorized access or breaches. It also helps the bank meet strict regulatory requirements imposed by German and EU laws on data residency and processing. Moreover, a controlled environment reduces risks of operational disruptions and supports reliable, auditable AI decision-making critical to banking trust.
