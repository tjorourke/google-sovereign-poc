A bank cares where its AI agents run because security, compliance, and data privacy are critical. Running AI agents in a controlled, sovereign environment ensures sensitive financial data is protected against breaches. It also guarantees adherence to local regulations like GDPR in Germany, preventing unauthorized data transfers abroad. Additionally, trusted infrastructure reduces risks of manipulation or errors that could impact customer trust and bank operations. Thus, the choice of platform directly affects a bank's integrity, reputation, and regulatory compliance.
