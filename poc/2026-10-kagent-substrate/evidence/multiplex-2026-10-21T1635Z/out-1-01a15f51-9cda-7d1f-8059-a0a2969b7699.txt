A bank cares where its AI agents run because of security, compliance, and data privacy. Banks handle sensitive financial and personal information that must be protected against breaches and misuse. Running AI agents within a trusted, secure environment like a sovereign platform with strict controls ensures data stays within jurisdictional boundaries, complying with regulations such as GDPR. Additionally, it reduces risks from external threats and guarantees operational reliability, maintaining customers' trust and safeguarding the bank's reputation.
