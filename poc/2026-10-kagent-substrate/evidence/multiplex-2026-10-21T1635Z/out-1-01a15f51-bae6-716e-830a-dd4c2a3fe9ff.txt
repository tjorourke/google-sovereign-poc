A bank cares where its AI agents run because security, compliance, and data privacy are critical. Financial data is highly sensitive and regulated. Running AI agents on trusted, sovereign platforms ensures data stays within approved geographic and jurisdictional boundaries, reducing risks of leaks or unauthorized access. It also enables the bank to comply with local laws such as GDPR. Additionally, trusted environments provide controlled infrastructure with monitoring and auditing, which protects customers and the bank’s reputation from cyber threats and operational failures.
