A bank cares where its AI agents run because ensuring data security and regulatory compliance is critical. Banks handle sensitive customer information subject to strict privacy laws like GDPR. Running AI agents on trusted, secure, and compliant infrastructure minimizes risks of data breaches and unauthorized access. Additionally, choice of location affects latency, performance, and integration with existing systems. Banks also require transparency and control over AI operations to meet audit and governance requirements, making the execution environment a key factor in safe, reliable AI deployment.
