A bank cares where its AI agents run because security and compliance are critical. Running AI within controlled environments, such as on dedicated sovereign platforms, ensures sensitive financial data stays protected under strict regulations like GDPR. It minimizes risks of data breaches or unauthorized access. Also, trusted infrastructure in specific jurisdictions meets legal requirements, enhances reliability, and maintains customer trust. Ensuring AI operates in secure, isolated environments helps the bank safeguard assets and reputation while complying with financial laws.
