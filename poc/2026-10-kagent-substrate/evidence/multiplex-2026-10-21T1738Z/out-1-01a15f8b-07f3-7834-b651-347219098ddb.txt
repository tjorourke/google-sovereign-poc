A bank cares where its AI agents run because of security, privacy, and regulatory compliance. Banks handle sensitive customer data that must be protected from breaches and unauthorized access. Running AI agents in trusted, controlled environments—such as secure, sovereign cloud platforms located in specific jurisdictions—helps ensure data residency requirements and legal regulations are met. This minimizes risks of data leakage, supports auditability, and maintains customer trust, which is critical for the bank's reputation and adherence to financial laws.
