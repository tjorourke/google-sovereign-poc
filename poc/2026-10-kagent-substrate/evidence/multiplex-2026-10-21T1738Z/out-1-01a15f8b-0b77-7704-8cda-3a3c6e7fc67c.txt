A bank cares deeply about where its AI agents run due to security, compliance, and reliability concerns. Running AI agents in secure, controlled environments protects sensitive financial data from breaches. Regulatory requirements often mandate data residency and strict privacy standards, necessitating location-specific cloud infrastructure. Additionally, trustworthy platforms ensure operational stability and consistent performance. For TrustUsBank, operating AI agents within its sovereign platform on Google Cloud Dedicated in Berlin aligns with legal obligations and guarantees data sovereignty while maintaining customer trust and safeguarding financial assets.
