A bank cares where its AI agents run because sensitive financial data and customer information must be kept secure and compliant with regulations like GDPR. Running AI agents on trusted, sovereign platforms ensures data protection within specified jurisdictions, reducing risks of breaches or unauthorized access. It also guarantees auditability and accountability. Furthermore, performance and reliability in critical banking operations depend on a stable, well-managed infrastructure, while regulatory bodies often require AI systems to be transparent and controllable within defined geographic boundaries.
