A bank cares where its AI agents run to ensure data security and regulatory compliance. Sensitive financial data must be processed in secure, trusted environments to prevent breaches and fraud. Banks are subject to strict laws requiring data residency within specific jurisdictions, like Germany or the EU. Running AI agents on dedicated, sovereign platforms like those in Berlin helps maintain customer privacy, control over data, and adherence to local regulations, which protects the bank's reputation and avoids costly penalties.
