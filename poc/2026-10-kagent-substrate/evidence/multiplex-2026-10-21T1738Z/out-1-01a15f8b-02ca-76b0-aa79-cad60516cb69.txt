A bank cares where its AI agents run because security, regulatory compliance, and data privacy are paramount. Banks handle sensitive financial information requiring strict protection against breaches and unauthorized access. Running AI agents in trusted, localized environments ensures adherence to data sovereignty laws and prevents data from leaving approved jurisdictions. Additionally, using secure, controlled platforms mitigates risks of manipulation or failures that could impact customer trust and financial stability. Reliable infrastructure also supports performance and uptime critical for banking operations.
