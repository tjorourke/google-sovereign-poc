A bank cares deeply about where its AI agents run because data security and regulatory compliance are paramount. Hosting AI on trusted, secure infrastructure within jurisdictional boundaries minimizes risks of data breaches, unauthorized access, or cross-border data transfer violations. It ensures that sensitive customer information is protected according to strict financial regulations, preserving customer trust and avoiding legal penalties. Additionally, local hosting supports better control and auditing of AI operations, which is critical for transparency and accountability in banking services.
