A bank cares where its AI agents run to ensure security, compliance, and data privacy. Running AI agents on trusted, sovereign platforms like dedicated cloud infrastructure in a specific region helps control access to sensitive financial data, meeting regulatory requirements such as GDPR. It reduces risks of data breaches and unauthorized access. Additionally, local execution enables low latency and reliability, crucial for real-time banking operations. Ultimately, location choice supports trustworthiness and operational integrity essential for banking services.
