A bank cares where its AI agents run because security and data privacy are paramount. Financial institutions handle sensitive customer information requiring strict compliance with regulations like GDPR. Running AI agents on trusted, sovereign cloud platforms within the bank’s jurisdiction helps ensure data stays protected and access is controlled. It also reduces risks from external threats or data leaks. Additionally, location impacts latency and performance, affecting real-time decisions and customer service quality. Overall, the choice ensures trust, compliance, and operational efficiency.
