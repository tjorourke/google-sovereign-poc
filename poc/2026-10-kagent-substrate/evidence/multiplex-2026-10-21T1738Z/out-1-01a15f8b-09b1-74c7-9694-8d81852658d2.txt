A bank cares where its AI agents run because security, data privacy, and regulatory compliance are paramount. Running AI agents in a trusted, controlled environment like a sovereign platform ensures sensitive financial data is protected from unauthorized access or breaches. It also guarantees that operations comply with local laws and regulations, such as GDPR. Additionally, reliable infrastructure in a specific location, like Berlin for a German bank, supports low-latency processing and auditability, which are critical for customer trust and operational integrity.
