A bank cares where its AI agents run because the location affects data security, compliance, and performance. Banks handle sensitive financial information, so they must ensure AI processing occurs in secure, trusted environments to protect customer privacy and prevent breaches. Regulatory requirements often mandate data residency and auditability, so AI agents must run in jurisdictions that comply with these laws. Additionally, running AI close to core banking infrastructure reduces latency, improving service responsiveness. Overall, location directly impacts risk management and regulatory adherence.
