A bank cares where its AI agents run to ensure data security, regulatory compliance, and operational reliability. Running AI in trusted environments like a sovereign platform within a local data center—such as Google Cloud Dedicated in Berlin—helps protect sensitive financial and personal data from unauthorized access or breaches. It also ensures adherence to local laws, such as GDPR in Germany. Additionally, controlled environments provide stable performance and reduce risks of downtime, ensuring consistent, trustworthy AI-driven banking services.
