A bank cares where its AI agents run because security and data privacy are paramount. Financial institutions handle sensitive customer data and must comply with strict regulations like GDPR. Running AI agents on secure, compliant infrastructure, such as a sovereign platform within a trusted geographic region, minimizes risks of data breaches and unauthorized access. It also ensures better control over data residency, transparency, and auditability. Additionally, performance and reliability of AI systems are crucial for banking operations, so a well-managed, local environment is preferred.
