A bank cares where its AI agents run because security and compliance are critical. Banks handle sensitive financial data and must protect it against breaches and unauthorized access. Running AI agents in trusted, controlled environments ensures data privacy and regulatory adherence, such as GDPR and banking laws. Location matters because certain jurisdictions have stricter data protection rules. Additionally, trusted platforms provide robust auditing and monitoring capabilities, helping banks maintain operational integrity and customer trust while minimizing risks of fraud or data loss.
