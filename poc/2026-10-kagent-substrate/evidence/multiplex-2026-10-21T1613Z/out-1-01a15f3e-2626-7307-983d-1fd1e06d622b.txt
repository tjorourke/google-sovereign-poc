A bank cares where its AI agents run mainly for data security, privacy, and regulatory compliance. Financial data is highly sensitive, so hosting AI agents on secure, local infrastructure like a sovereign cloud within the country helps protect against unauthorized access and data breaches. Additionally, banks must comply with strict financial regulations requiring data residency and auditability. Running AI agents in trusted environments ensures operational consistency, resilience, and control, which is crucial to maintaining customer trust and meeting legal obligations.
