A bank cares where its AI agents run because security, data privacy, and regulatory compliance are paramount. Hosting AI agents on trusted, secure infrastructure—such as a sovereign platform in Germany—ensures sensitive financial data is protected from unauthorized access. It also helps meet strict German and EU regulations on data residency and processing. Moreover, reliable performance and controlled environments minimize risks of errors or breaches that could harm customers or the bank's reputation. This trust in hosting locations is crucial for safe AI deployment in banking.
