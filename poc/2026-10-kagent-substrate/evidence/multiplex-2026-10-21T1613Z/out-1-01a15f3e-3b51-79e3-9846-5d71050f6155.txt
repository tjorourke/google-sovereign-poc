A bank cares where its AI agents run because security, privacy, and regulatory compliance are critical. Sensitive financial data must be protected against unauthorized access and cyberattacks. Running AI agents in a trusted, controlled environment like a sovereign platform ensures data stays within jurisdictional boundaries, complying with data protection laws like GDPR. Additionally, it guarantees operational reliability and auditability, which are essential for maintaining customer trust and meeting banking regulations. Location affects risk management and reputation.
