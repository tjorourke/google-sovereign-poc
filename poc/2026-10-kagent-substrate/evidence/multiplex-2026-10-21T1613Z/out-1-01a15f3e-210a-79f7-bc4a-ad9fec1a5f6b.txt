A bank cares where its AI agents run primarily to ensure data security, regulatory compliance, and operational reliability. Financial data is highly sensitive, so AI systems must operate in secure, controlled environments to prevent breaches and unauthorized access. Additionally, banking regulations often require data to remain within specific jurisdictions, like Germany or the EU, to comply with privacy laws such as GDPR. Running AI agents in trusted, local infrastructure also supports system stability and performance, which is critical for customer trust and business continuity.
