A bank cares where its AI agents run because data security, privacy, and regulatory compliance are critical in banking. Hosting AI agents on trusted, sovereign platforms ensures customer information is protected under local laws, reducing risks of breaches or misuse. It also guarantees operational reliability and control, essential for financial stability. Banks must comply with strict regulations like GDPR, requiring data to stay within certain jurisdictions. Choosing the right environment helps maintain trust, safeguard assets, and meet compliance mandates.
