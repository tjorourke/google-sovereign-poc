A bank cares deeply about where its AI agents run because of security, compliance, and data privacy. Banks handle sensitive financial information that must be protected against breaches and unauthorized access. Running AI agents in trusted, controlled environments, like a sovereign platform within a specific jurisdiction, ensures adherence to local regulations and data protection laws. It also helps maintain customer trust by guaranteeing that their data is processed safely and transparently, reducing risks related to fraud, legal penalties, and reputational damage.
