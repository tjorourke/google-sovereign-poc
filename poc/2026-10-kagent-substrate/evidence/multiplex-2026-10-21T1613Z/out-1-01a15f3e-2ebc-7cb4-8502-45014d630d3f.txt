A bank cares where its AI agents run primarily for security, compliance, and data sovereignty. Banks handle sensitive financial and personal data, so running AI within a secure, controlled environment reduces risks of data breaches. Regulatory requirements often mandate that data and processing remain within specific geographic areas, such as Germany or the EU, to comply with privacy laws like GDPR. Additionally, using trusted cloud providers with robust infrastructure ensures reliability, performance, and auditability, all crucial for maintaining customer trust and operational integrity.
