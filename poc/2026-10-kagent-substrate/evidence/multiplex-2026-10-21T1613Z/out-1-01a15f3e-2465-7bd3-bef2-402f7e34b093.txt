A bank cares where its AI agents run because security, data privacy, and regulatory compliance are critical. Running AI on secure, trusted infrastructure minimizes risks of data breaches. Banks handle sensitive customer information subject to strict regulations like GDPR, so location impacts legal compliance. Using dedicated, sovereign platforms in regions like Berlin ensures data residency and control. Additionally, reliable, low-latency environments improve AI performance, enabling better customer service and risk management. Overall, where AI runs directly affects a bank’s trustworthiness and operational integrity.
