A bank cares where its AI agents run because security, data privacy, and regulatory compliance are critical. Running AI on trusted, secure infrastructure—like a sovereign platform inside a dedicated cloud in Germany—ensures sensitive financial data stays protected under strict local laws. It minimizes risks of data breaches and unauthorized access. Additionally, the bank must meet regulatory requirements such as GDPR, which dictate where and how data can be processed. Reliable, controlled environments also guarantee performance stability and operational transparency.
