A bank cares deeply about where its AI agents run because of security, data privacy, and regulatory compliance. Banks handle sensitive financial and personal customer data, so AI systems must operate in secure, trusted environments to prevent unauthorized access or breaches. Regulatory rules often require data to stay within certain geographic or jurisdictional boundaries, like Germany or the EU. Running AI agents on dedicated, local infrastructure helps ensure compliance, reduces risks, and maintains customer trust in the bank’s digital services.
