A bank cares where its AI agents run because security, data privacy, and compliance are critical in banking. Running AI within a secure, controlled environment, like a trusted cloud platform, ensures protection against cyber threats and unauthorized access to sensitive financial data. It also helps meet regulatory requirements, such as data residency laws and auditability. Moreover, performance and reliability are vital for customer trust, so choosing a stable infrastructure ensures AI services operate efficiently and securely, safeguarding the bank's reputation and clients' assets.
